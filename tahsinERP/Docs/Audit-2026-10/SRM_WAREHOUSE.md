I also checked Scripts/20260519_DLOC_FIFO_Core_Migration.sql for unique indexes and Views/Shared/_Layout.cshtml lines 925-960.

**Key structural facts:**
- Stock is held in two parallel stores. **PRODUCT_STOCKS** holds a single `Amount` per warehouse+product. The **DLOC ledger** is spread over INCOME_LOTS, LOT_LOCATIONS and EXPENSE_PRODUCT_ALLOCATIONS.
- The DLOC tables are written through raw SQL (`DlocFifoDataAccess`, `LocationLotStockLedger`), and also through EF (`WarehouseFlowService.ReverseProductionIncome`).
- No table has a rowversion.
- PRODUCT_STOCKS has no unique (WHID, ProductID) constraint. `GetOrCreateStockRow` and `NormalizeWarehouseStocksAsync` exist to merge the duplicates that result.
- Documents are linked by text markers in `Description` and `Comment` (`EXPENSE_ID:n`, `EXPENS ID: n`, `IncomeID:n`, `ProductionID:n`, `/SalesInvoices/Details/n`), not by foreign keys.

## Cross-module notes (shared / core layer)

- **Permission enforcement:** many controllers rely on view-level `Response.Redirect`. A `[Permission("Module", Action)]` filter, registered in FilterConfig next to LogActionFilter and applied to every controller, would close SRM-S01, S02 and S03 across the whole app.
- **Atomic stock API:** PRODUCT_STOCKS is also mutated from MES (ProductProductionController, ProductionConsumptionService), CRM sales invoices (DecreaseSalesStock) and PContracts/PInvoices. The SRM-B01 fix must go into the single `StockLedger` that all of them call, plus a DB unique index on PRODUCT_STOCKS(WHID, ProductID).
- **Text-marker linking** (`EXPENSE_ID:`, `ProductionID:`, `/SalesInvoices/Details/`, `IncomeID:`) is a cross-module design issue (SRM, MES, CRM). Plan FK columns plus a backfill script.
- **Document numbering:** use one `DocNumberService` for INCOMES, EXPENSES, TRNSF, PROD, INV and sales.
- **Reference-data cache** (UNITS, CURRENCIES, WAREHOUSES and warehouse roles): used by almost every module.
- **_Layout per-request queries** (SRM-P20): the notification dot queries APP_TASKS and INCOMES on every page.
- **SoftDeleteReferenceService** nulls historical FKs for products (BOM_LINES.ComponentID, NORMVERSIONS.ProductID, which destroys BOMs), warehouses, units and currencies. Review it with the Engineering, HR and Finance owners. Soft delete should normally **not** rewrite history.
- **LogActionFilter volume** makes USERLOGS large. The module misuses it as a business lock (SRM-P07).
- **Attachments stored as varbinary** in WRHS_*_DOCS (and probably in other modules) inflate the DB and EF loads. Plan a shared AttachmentService with file storage.
- **CDN script hygiene** (unpinned chart.js, duplicate jQuery) probably recurs in other modules' views.
- **TransactionScope vs BeginTransaction** are mixed on the same DbContext (WarehouseFlowService.cs:565, :801, :914 vs the controllers). Standardise on the caller owning `db.Database.BeginTransaction()`.
